=== 1. Data (REAL) ===
source: Amazon Reviews 2023 fragrance reviews about the smell, matched to Fragrantica accords.
one row per person: 264 people, each described by 20 accord taste scores (no outcome: clustering)
taste score = average of (stars - own average) over the person's perfumes with that accord

=== 2. Split ===
30% of each person's ratings held out: train profiles from 1,969 ratings, test profiles from 820
