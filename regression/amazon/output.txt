=== 1. Data (REAL) ===
source: Amazon Reviews 2023 (McAuley Lab), fragrance reviews 2003-2023, filtered to reviews about
the smell and matched to Fragrantica accords (Kaggle). See data/README.md.
one row per (person, perfume): 2,789 ratings, 264 people, 1,725 perfumes
outcome: rating (1-5 stars)  <- OUTCOME
features (65): person_avg, person_n (from the person's other training ratings), fra_rating,
  fra_log_count (Fragrantica), acc_* (perfume has accord), taste_* (person's liking of accord vs own avg),
  match_* = taste x accord, match_total. Accords: woody, sweet, citrus, fruity, aromatic, powdery, white floral, floral, warm spicy, fresh spicy, vanilla, amber, fresh, musky, green, rose, lavender, patchouli, aquatic, caramel

=== 2. Split ===
30% of each person's ratings held out (seed 0): 1,969 train / 820 test
