=== 1. Data (REAL, generosity controlled) ===
Amazon fragrance ratings about the smell, matched to Fragrantica: 2,789 ratings, 264 people
normal = person's training average, pulled toward the overall average by 3 pseudo-ratings
outcome: target = stars - normal  <- OUTCOME  (positive = they liked it more than usual)
features (64): person_n, fra_rating, fra_log_count, acc_*, ctaste_* (liking of accord vs own normal), cmatch_*
target: mean +0.00, sd 1.10 (train)

=== 2. Split ===
30% of each person's ratings held out (seed 0): 1,969 train / 820 test

=== 3. Fit (ridge, alpha by CV grouped by person) ===
alpha 1e+03; training R^2 0.020, adjusted R^2 -0.013
biggest effects (stars above normal per 1 sd of the feature):
fra_log_count        0.078
cmatch_total         0.034
ctaste_musky        -0.018
ctaste_warm spicy   -0.014
cmatch_powdery       0.014
acc_citrus           0.013
acc_white floral     0.012
cmatch_woody         0.011
acc_powdery          0.010
cmatch_fruity       -0.010
