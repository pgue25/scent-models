=== 1. Data (REAL, product level) ===
source: Fragrantica perfumes via Kaggle archive (fra_perfumes.csv), scraped by the dataset author.
one row per perfume with 50+ ratings: 24,486 perfumes, 1,001 brands
outcome: rating = Fragrantica average rating (1-5)  <- OUTCOME
features: 20 accord flags (woody, citrus, sweet, aromatic, powdery, fruity, floral, warm spicy, white floral, amber, fresh spicy, vanilla, musky, green, fresh, rose, earthy, leather, patchouli, aquatic), gender (women / men / unisex), log number of ratings
no per-user data: this model cannot recommend for a specific person

=== 2. Split ===
random 70/30 split of perfumes (seed 0): 17,314 train / 7,172 test
