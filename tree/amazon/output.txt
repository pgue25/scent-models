=== 1. Data (REAL) ===
source: Amazon Reviews 2023 fragrance reviews about the smell, matched to Fragrantica accords.
one row per (person, perfume): 2,789 ratings, 264 people, 1,725 perfumes
outcome: liked = rating 4 or 5  <- OUTCOME;  features: same 65 as the regression (see regression/amazon)
share liked: 0.772

=== 2. Split ===
30% of each person's ratings held out (seed 0): 1,969 train / 820 test
